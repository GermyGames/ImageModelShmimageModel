# Dorito Pope Cathedral

- Native canvas of 256 × 320 pixels
- Custom 62-color palette with blue stone, lime stained glass, and red-orange firelight
- Subject reference in `source/reference.jpg`

The portrait supplies the chip mitre, raised Mountain Dew bottle, and liturgical vestments. The character references guide the small face and slender figure. A narrow raised sleeve, floor-length cassock, and embroidered stole catch lime window light and warm Doritos-red firelight. The Pope stands directly behind the lectern. Triangular insignia link the rose window, banners, vestment, lectern, and aisle. Both gargoyles sit forward of their pillars, and two standing and two kneeling worshippers have clearly articulated limbs and visible floor around their feet.

The style references guide the cyan and crimson lighting, moonlit Gothic architecture, monumental silhouettes, and weathered stone. The composition is drawn on the native pixel grid in `source/draw.py`.

The four build passes sit in `build/steps/`. The export includes the native PNG, an integer 4× preview, the palette, and a timelapse with the saved revisions.
